## 2. Performance Check
- **Old implementation:** Lua called per-sample (1,024 calls/block).
- **New implementation:** Lua called once per block with zero-allocation pointer access. 
- **Result:** We went from "Space Heater" to "Pro Tools" efficiency.

- **Segfaults:** Mitigated by `GrokGodModeHelper` and proper ownership tracking.
