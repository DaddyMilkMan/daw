## Preset Quality Standards

Every preset must be:

### ✅ Production Ready
- Gain-staged properly (no clipping)
- EQ-balanced (sits in mix)
- Effects-enhanced (reverb, delay included)
- Velocity-responsive (expressive playing)

### ✅ Musical & Useful
- Not just "cool sounds" but usable instruments
- Fits specific genres/contexts
- Has musical range (bass, lead, pad, etc.)

### ✅ Expertly Designed
- By professional sound designers
- Tested in real productions
- Iterated until perfect

### ✅ Well-Organized
- Named descriptively (not "Patch 001")
- Tagged by genre, type, mood
- Searchable: "dark reese bass", "bright pad", etc.

### ✅ Optimized
- Minimal CPU usage
- RT-safe (no xruns)
- Fast preset loading
